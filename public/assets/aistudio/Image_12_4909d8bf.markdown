## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an evocative, editorial experience for a cultural monograph examining Hanoi's urban memory through its quintessential dish: phở. The design language marries the tactile warmth of handcrafted Vietnamese artisanal paper (giấy dó) with the refined, spacious cadence of contemporary literary journalism.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience:** Cultured urban readers, culinary enthusiasts, travelers, literary collectors, and the Vietnamese diaspora seeking authentic cultural depth over superficial tourist motifs.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Contemplative, nostalgic, sensory, grounded, and dignified. It balances the steam of early-morning street-corner stalls with rigorous cultural observation.
- **Visual Movement:** Editorial Modernist meets Tactile Nostalgia. Visuals evoke watercolor sketches, woodblock impressions, warm aged stock, and crisp, deliberate typography.

## Colors
The palette draws straight from Hanoi's streetscape, architectural patina, and culinary chemistry:

- **Primary (`#8C4027` - Nâu gốm Bát Tràng):** Terracotta kiln glaze and aged brickwork. Anchors primary calls-to-action, active chapter markers, and critical thematic accents.
- **Secondary (`#DEAA48` - Vàng hổ phách nước dùng):** Luminous amber broth infused with charred ginger and star anise. Used for star ratings, contextual highlights, chapter badges, and warm sensory accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C94A3D` - Đỏ ghế nhựa vỉa hè):** The ubiquitous, lively vermilion of street-side stools and paper stamps. Applied sparingly for promotional ribbons, flash alerts, and pull-quote flourishes.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#F6EFE6` - Giấy dó ngà nhạt):** Raw handmade mulberry paper providing an eye-resting, non-glare editorial canvas.
- **Neutral Container (`#FAF6F0` - Trắng kem mộc):** Bleached raw linen surface tier for elevated cards, excerpt plaques, and quotation boards.
- **Neutral Text & Headings (`#2A221E` - Mực in thớt nghiến):** Deep, dense sumi-ink brown-black, offering rich readability without the harsh synthetic edge of pure digital black.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets the balance between cultural gravitas and crisp mobile legibility:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the intellectual weight of classic publishing, high-end monographs, and historical reflection. Italic styles are strictly reserved for book passages, historical citations, and culinary terminology.
- **Body & Controls (Be Vietnam Pro):** Tailor-made for flawless Vietnamese diacritic rendering with open apertures, distinct tones, and optimal x-height for long-form reading on handheld devices.
- **Editorial Microcopy:** Uppercase tracked labels (`label-caps`) simulate woodblock prints and archival chapter labeling.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column editorial grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single fluid column on mobile screens:

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** Max container width 1200px, flanked by generous `margin` (3rem / 48px) and `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Long-form excerpt copy is constrained to an 8-column reading spine (max 720px) to preserve literary ergonomics.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with 2rem margins, enabling side-by-side book previews and author reflections.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `margin-mobile` (1.25rem / 20px) and `gutter-mobile` (1rem / 16px).
- **Rhythm:** Generous vertical rhythm utilizing `space-2xl` between major chapters/sections creates breathing room reminiscent of unbound archival books.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through organic, tactile layering rather than cold digital drop shadows:

- **Surface Tonal Stacking:** Visual separation relies on layered cards (`#FAF6F0`) resting against the natural paper base (`#F6EFE6`).
- **Warm Mineral Borders:** Surfaces use delicate, tactile borders: `1px solid rgba(42, 34, 30, 0.12)`. Active, focused, or featured items shift to `1px solid rgba(140, 64, 39, 0.45)`.
- **Soft Amber-Tinted Diffusion:** Elevated elements (such as book preview popovers, purchase sticky bars, or floating navigation) employ an amber-tinted shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(42, 34, 30, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(222, 170, 72, 0.06)`. Pure synthetic gray shadows are disallowed.

## Shapes
Shapes adhere to a soft, artisanal geometry (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Cards & Plates:** Subtly rounded corners (`4px` to `8px`) evoke trimmed book cardstock, postcard edges, and book jackets without appearing juvenile or overly digital.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Fixed at `4px` corner radius, retaining the structured feel of classic publisher press seals.
- **Media & Sketches:** Watercolor imagery and historical photographs feature crisp, minimal rounding (`4px` to `6px`) paired with subtle inner borders replicating paper matting.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Đặt sách ngay" / "Thưởng thức mục lục"):** Solid Bát Tràng Terracotta (`#8C4027`) background, warm linen text (`#FAF6F0`), 4px border radius. Hover state transitions to darkened terracotta (`#75341F`) with a slight upward translation of 1px.
- **Secondary CTA ("Đọc thử chương 1"):** Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in ink brown (`#2A221E`), text `#2A221E`. On hover: fills with `#2A221E` and inverts text to `#FAF6F0`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Link:** Raw amber text (`#8C4027`) with a subtle terracotta bottom stroke and right arrow glyph (`→`).

### 2. Editorial Cards & Chapter Highlights
- **Card Base:** Linen background (`#FAF6F0`), 1px low-contrast border (`rgba(42, 34, 30, 0.1)`), 8px border radius, padded with `space-lg` to `space-xl`.
- **Chapter Monograph Tag:** Displays an archival index number (e.g., *CHƯƠNG III*) using `label-caps` in `#DEAA48`.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Features a Playfair Display title, brief excerpt in Be Vietnam Pro body text, and an illustrated watercolor spot.

### 3. Excerpt & Pull-Quote Plaques
- Inset styling with a 3px vertical accent bar on the left in `#8C4027`.
- Background set to a soft tint (`rgba(222, 170, 72, 0.08)`).
- Text rendered in `subhead-serif` (Playfair Display Italic), accompanied by author and edition attribution in `label-caps`.

### 4. Input Fields & Order Form
- **Form Containers:** Framed inside `#FAF6F0` modules.
- **Text Inputs:** `#FAF6F0` background with `1px solid rgba(42, 34, 30, 0.2)` border, 4px border radius, vertical padding `0.75rem`, horizontal padding `1rem`. Focus state features a sharp terracotta highlight (`1.5px solid #8C4027`) without glowing outlines.
- **Placeholder:** `#2A221E` at 45% opacity.

### 5. Chips, Tags & Edition Badges
- **Limited Hardcover Edition Tag:** Vermilion background (`#C94A3D`), white text, 2px border radius, tracked uppercase lettering.
- **Flavor & Locality Chips (e.g., "Phở Bát Đàn", "Hương hồi quế"):** Linen surface (`#FAF6F0`), bordered in `rgba(42, 34, 30, 0.15)`, text `#2A221E`.

### 6. Interactive Book Spec & Map Accordion
- Collapsible list rows separated by faint hairline rules (`1px solid rgba(42, 34, 30, 0.08)`).
- Expand/collapse indicators utilize minimal typography glyphs (`+` / `−`) set in bold serif.
